Hypothesis tracking

The beliefs behind each TPP line, who owns them, and the agents watching for evidence.

Hypotheses
What it isA falsifiable statement about the market at launch, tied to one TPP line. Confidence starts at the owner's judgment.
Who owns itThe function responsible for that TPP line: Clinical for comparator and endpoints, Access for pricing, Forecasting for testing and demand, Operations for timeline.
How it updatesAttached agents surface signals; the Implication agent proposes a move; the owner applies or dismisses. Crossing the threshold reopens the position.

Signals

Only items that touch a hypothesis. Each carries the agent that found it and a proposed move.

Signals
What it isA detectable change in the world or in Astellas's own knowledge, with a one-line "so what" for this decision.
Who owns itThe agent that found it is named on each signal; the hypothesis owner decides whether to apply the proposed move.
How the move is sizedEvidence strength × source reliability × relevance. A registry Phase 3 start is strong; an investor-call remark is weak. Overrides are logged.

Decision workbench

Proposed decisions
What it isDecisions proposed from combinations of hypothesis movement — not a fixed list. Each names the trigger that fired, the evidence, and the options.
Who owns itThe owner of the affected TPP line frames and takes the decision; contributors see the same chain in their view.
How it worksEvery hypothesis carries a "what would change my mind" trigger. When triggers fire together, a decision pattern proposes options and the Simulation agent prices them with today's confidences.

Positions

The TPP is six positions. Each is a sentence, an owner, a confidence, and a sign-off.

ProvisionalProposed by the owner; the evidence supports it today but the team has not signed off.
LockedThe owner signed off and contributors acknowledged. Versioned with the confidences and ranges at that moment.
ReopenedA hypothesis behind it crossed its threshold since the lock. The sign-off no longer stands by default.

Export freezes the six positions with their hypotheses, the signals that moved them, the alternatives considered and the scenario ranges as a versioned TPP record.

Agent Studio

Two kinds of knowledge on either side, the agents in between. Attach them to hypotheses, change their cadence, or draft a new one.

Agents
Always-onScan external sources on a fixed cycle (default every 6 hours) with change detection, so only deltas are processed.
DailyIndex Astellas's own material — ad boards, field notes, RWE, portfolio memory — once a day, with medical validation before anything surfaces.
On eventRun when something happens: a signal arrives (Implication), a confidence changes (Simulation), a brief is requested, or any claim is made (Guardrail).
